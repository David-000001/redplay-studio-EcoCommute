# UM EcoCommute — A Smart Campus Mobility and Climate Awareness Application

RedPlay Studio · Tutorial Group 9 · WIA2007 · Universiti Malaya · Semester 1, 2026/2027

Project lead: Avijit Chandra Dey. Revised 10 November 2026. The earlier leader-reported SDG approval applied to the previous proposal. Lecturer acceptance of this revised topic has not been documented.

## Purpose

Help UM students understand how travel mode and distance affect their estimated commuting footprint. Validate the need with student commuters and willing campus mobility stakeholders.

## Student experience

A student records a journey, compares modes for the same distance, reviews personal trends and completes a climate lesson, quiz or challenge. An administrator can review anonymized campus mobility summaries. Students supply trip details manually. The MVP does not require GPS tracking, ticket sales or real-time bus feeds.
