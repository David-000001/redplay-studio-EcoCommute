# Climate impact and SDG 13.3 rationale (draft)

## Goal
Make climate change consequences of food waste understandable, while giving authorized UM stakeholders transparent *indicative* monitoring metrics.

## Indicators
- **Verified portions recovered**: counted after vendor collection confirmation.
- **Estimated recovered mass (kg)**: entered/confirmed by vendor, with estimation confidence flagged.
- **Indicative emissions associated with recovered food (kg CO₂e)**: estimated mass × documented food-category lifecycle emission factor.

**Important:** lifecycle emissions associated with food ≠ net emissions avoided through donation. Any avoided-emissions estimate would additionally need a baseline, disposal assumptions, replacement effects and other considerations. Never label the simpler multiplication “verified CO₂e saved.”

## Validation plan
Select credible, cited food-specific emission factors, record factor unit/geography/year, perform sensitivity checks, and distinguish illustrative from observed totals. Test basic climate-understanding before/after viewing app climate insights, without claiming causation from an uncontrolled convenience sample.

## SDG 13.3 mapping
- Climate education: source-backed bite-size insights and user-comprehension study.
- Awareness: contextual estimated food-climate footprint and method explanation.
- Institutional capacity: authorized, exportable aggregate monitoring dashboard.

## Safety
Do not infer food safety from AI pictures. Vendors remain responsible for declared preparation/handling and legal requirements.
